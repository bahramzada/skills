# Evidence synthesis

Adapted from upstream knowledge-synthesis; see NOTICE and sources.lock.json.

Merge findings by the user's question and exact claim, rather than by website or worker. Deduplicate copied announcements/shared underlying data. Preserve different entities, editions, time periods, populations and genuinely contradictory statements.

For each material claim:

1. Identify the exact object, attribute, unit, scope and period.
2. Compare candidate values to their actual supporting content.
3. Evaluate authority for this claim, specificity, effective date and supersession.
4. Explain a selected value and retain material counterevidence.
5. Leave unresolved uncertainty visible and propagate it into recommendations.

Use qualitative, claim-specific verification labels. Do not manufacture reliability percentages, weighted confidence formulas or independent confirmations from multiple copies of the same evidence.

Attach source IDs to important facts. In mixed-source rows, associate each value with its supporting source. The final registry maps IDs to specific documents/URLs, authors/organizations, relevant dates and supported information. Offline supplied excerpts are explicitly distinct from live-page verification. Remove unused registry entries; keep failed leads as gaps.

Lead with the answer and evidence-based implications. Keep recommendations and assumptions separate from established facts. Preserve necessary detail and the user's structure; do not summarize away unresolved questions or missing work to appear complete.
