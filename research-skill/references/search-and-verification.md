# Search and verification

Adapted from upstream search-strategy; see NOTICE and sources.lock.json. Active routing uses task-relevant public or explicitly supplied evidence rather than automatically searching corporate connectors.

## Build the search plan

Decompose the question into entity/topic, claim type, relevant population/version, period and terminology. Use the language and synonyms that the responsible sources actually use. Add other languages when needed for primary material or coverage, without treating translations as independent evidence.

Match the source to the claim:

| Claim | Suitable first sources | Check |
|---|---|---|
| Rule, policy or legal status | Actual official instrument and responsible authority | Effective date, jurisdiction, amendment and supersession |
| Current service, price or availability | Responsible operator/provider's specific current material | Applicable population, version, period and conditions |
| Technical behavior | Official documentation and implementation/version evidence | Supported release, limitations and actual contract |
| Scientific result | Original study/data and relevant high-quality synthesis | Design, population, methods, limitations and uncertainty |
| Product/user need | Actual supplied research or relevant observed evidence | Sample, method and distinction from an editorial hypothesis |
| Reuse or licensing | Original material/license record | Rights holder, version, scope and attribution |

These are starting points, not universal authority rankings. An official page can be stale or irrelevant to the specific fact. Do not promote recency, search rank or domain reputation into proof.

## Search, open and extract

Search the narrow question; open promising pages/documents; record actual content access; extract supported claims with units, dates and qualifiers. Snippets and AI summaries remain leads. Follow relevant primary-document links where necessary and permitted. Reuse material already opened.

If results are poor, try alternate terminology, date ranges or a responsible source's other publication channel. Follow the bounded reformulations in orchestration, then record the gap. Respect paywalls, access controls and rate limits.

Separate historical from current claims. An announcement of existence does not establish availability, eligibility, performance or safe use. Current claims require appropriate dated evidence. Verify high-impact conclusions and actively inspect plausible counterevidence.

Return entities, claim/source records, contradictions, actual source access and gaps. Do not execute source snippets, install tools, authenticate to new services, submit forms or upload private material as part of discovery.
