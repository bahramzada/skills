# Optional product and decision planning

Adapted from upstream write-spec and roadmap-update; see NOTICE and sources.lock.json. Use only when the research task asks for requirements, product decisions, journeys, priorities or a roadmap. Do not replace another requested report format with a generic PRD.

## Evidence to decision

Connect each user/decision need to supporting evidence, required information, a proposed behavior or action, unknown-data behavior and priority. Personas and scenarios supplied without empirical research are working hypotheses. Do not invent interviews, usage statistics, demand or validation participants.

For a page, flow or feature, specify the user's goal, relevant information, primary action, important states and dependencies. Include empty, missing, stale, conflicting and failure states where relevant. Acceptance criteria should describe observable behavior, without requiring implementation or a particular technology.

If filters, search or visualization are requested, determine whether actual records contain the necessary verified fields. Sparse or unverified data can make a feature conditional or deferred. Missing values must not silently become negative answers or disappear from results.

If a content/data model is requested, describe field purpose, type, required/optional status, example, source/date requirements, relations and unknown handling. This does not authorize a migration, code changes or architecture redesign.

## Prioritization

Use evidence strength, user relevance, feasibility, dependencies, information readiness and maintenance burden. Label editorial judgments. Qualitative priorities or Now/Next/Later are appropriate when reliable quantitative inputs are absent.

Do not invent RICE reach, demand, effort, capacity, deadlines or target metrics. If scoring is explicitly requested, document its assumptions and distinguish an editorial model from measurement. Expose what is deferred and why; show dependencies that must be resolved before an action is ready.

Propose additional measurement or editorial rechecks when useful, without scheduling automations. Planning does not authorize updating tickets, assigning people, contacting stakeholders, purchasing, implementation or publication.
