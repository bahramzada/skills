# Compare supplied research reports

Use when the user supplies reports and requests comparison or consolidated synthesis. Record the actual number and identity of reports supplied, their stated authors/AI/models when relevant, dates, browsing status and completeness. Never guess unknown identity or treat missing reports as completed work.

1. Normalize questions, scope, entities, units, periods and qualifiers. Map local entity/source IDs to canonical ones.
2. Compare field-level claims and their actual supporting documents. Multiple reports citing one underlying source represent one evidence lineage.
3. Separate supported agreement, unsupported agreement, contradictions and missing data. Majority vote, fluency or confidence labels do not establish truth.
4. Open important original sources with permitted tools when verification is possible. For offline copies/excerpts, preserve provenance and explicitly state that live pages were not opened.
5. Resolve values by claim-specific evidence, or retain the conflict. Never fill blanks from model memory.
6. Produce the user's requested consolidated format plus a concise comparison and reconciliation table. State what remains incomplete or unverified.

Same-model roles or internal workers do not establish independent AI runs or independent empirical replication. Supplied reports do not authorize external uploads, private-connector searches or modification of originals.
