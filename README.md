### Research you can trace

Research Skill takes a question from initial scope to a source-backed answer. It adapts to the topic, language, and requested format while keeping evidence and uncertainty visible.

- **Verify:** Find relevant sources and check important claims.
- **Synthesize:** Connect findings, compare reports, and surface contradictions or gaps.
- **Plan:** Translate research into requirements and priorities when requested.
- **Review:** Check coverage, attribution, and limitations before delivery.

> **Try it:** “Compare these two market reports, verify their key claims, and explain where their conclusions differ.”
