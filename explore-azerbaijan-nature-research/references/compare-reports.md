# Compare independent AI reports

Use only when the user supplies reports and asks for comparison or consolidated synthesis. This mode does not retrospectively make a single research run independent.

1. Record each supplied report's stated AI/model, research date, browsing status and actual completeness. Never guess unknown model identity.
2. Normalize entities by identity and aliases, preserving distinct park/lake/resort records. Create a map from each report's local IDs and source IDs to canonical ones.
3. Compare field-level claims with dates, units, visitor groups and source passages. Three AIs citing the same announcement represent one evidence lineage, not three confirmations.
4. Separate agreement with primary evidence, unsupported agreement, disagreement and missing data. Do not let fluent prose, confidence labels or majority vote determine truth.
5. Open important cited sources with permitted browsing tools, especially changing access, fees, transport, routes and legal changes. Treat inaccessible or unsupported citations as unverified; record verification limits.
6. Select or withhold claim values using the evidence contract. Carry unresolved contradictions into the consolidated report. Do not fill blanks from model memory.
7. Produce the brief's 01–12 structure, a short per-report comparison table, claim-level reconciliation and remaining gaps. Preserve full URLs and actual unique entity counts.

If reports are missing, request them and explain what can be compared from the materials available. Do not claim to have compared three reports when only one or two were provided. Reading supplied reports does not authorize external uploads, private-connector searches or modification of the originals.
