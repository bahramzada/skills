# Inventory, profiles and routes

This domain-specific module implements the user's brief; it is not a claim that the upstream sources contain tourism expertise.

## Taxonomy and coverage

Separate three dimensions: legal/protection status, physical landscape and visitor activity. Define a primary category for catalogue organization; use additional tags and relations for overlapping properties. Assess national parks, state nature reserves, other relevant natural/landscape parks, lakes, waterfalls and routes. Explain treatment of forests, valleys, canyons, mountains, natural monuments, mud volcanoes and reservoirs.

Track coverage by the geography actually used. Administrative districts, economic regions and editorial tourism directions must not be interchangeable. Explicitly track Naxçıvan, Qarabağ, Şərqi Zəngəzur and under-documented areas. An official park crossing district borders remains one place with multiple district relations.

The 50–70-place planning target is not an official inventory count. Report the official park/reserve completeness separately from the broader editorial selection. Give source-backed or unknown access status to every inventory row. Routes occupy section 05 rather than inflating place totals.

## Entity relationships

Maintain park → contained lake; lake → trail; route → multiple stops; place → multiple districts/region relationships. Distinguish Göygöl National Park from Göygöl lake and Şahdağ National Park from the tourism complex. A historical-cultural reserve is not a state nature reserve merely because both names contain a word equivalent to reserve.

Deduplicate using legal identity, geographic relation, aliases and source evidence. Give unmatched or disputed identities an explicit reconciliation note. Confirm every cross-reference resolves before final assembly.

## Profiles

Select 8–10 supported places spanning categories and regions. Preserve every A–H group in section 04 of the brief:

| Group | Required focus |
|---|---|
| A. Kimlik | Stable identity, AZ/EN/RU names, category/status, 40–70-word AZ card and sourced introduction |
| B. Əsas faktlar | Entity-appropriate indicators with units and sources |
| C. Xəritə | Coordinates, point meaning, precision and evidence |
| D. Ziyarət | Access, permits, guide, hours, dated fees and visitor categories |
| E. Nəqliyyat | Approach origin, public/private transport, walking section, dated road information |
| F. Mövsüm və fəaliyyət | Supported activities, suitable periods and limitations |
| G. İnfrastruktur | Facilities, family visit and physical accessibility; preserve unknowns |
| H. Təbiət və əlaqəli məzmun | Landscape, biodiversity, related IDs, 3–5 practical FAQs and photo licensing |

Finish each profile with gaps and actual verification date. A source for one field does not support all the profile's other fields. Do not describe a place as universally accessible or family-safe from marketing language alone.

## Routes

Aim for 10–15 evidenced routes across regions/difficulties/durations. Preserve the brief's fields: ID/name, start/end, stop IDs, route shape, distance basis, walking time, separate driving approach, ascent/descent, sourced difficulty, months, marking, guide/permission, water/infrastructure, map/GPX if available, current access and unknowns.

Prefer a supported shorter set to fabricated or duplicate routes. A commercial itinerary is evidence of that operator's offered program at a particular date, not an officially maintained public trail. Editorial combinations need the exact unverified label from the evidence contract and cannot be recommended as confirmed traversable paths.

## Count reconciliation

Count distinct canonical place IDs and route IDs separately. Report how many profiles and MVP selections were actually completed. Include a clear regional-gap table even when the selection reaches the numerical target.
